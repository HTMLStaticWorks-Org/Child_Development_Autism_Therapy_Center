Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common parent questions

Learn more about how our clinical team supports your child's developmental path.

What age groups does Bright Path support?
We provide specialized developmental care for infants, toddlers, and children from 12 months through 16 years of age, emphasizing early intervention and school transition.
Do we need a formal medical referral before booking?
No doctor referral is required to schedule an initial consultation or developmental screening. We welcome all families seeking guidance.
Can parents observe or join therapy sessions?
Yes, caregiver participation is at the core of our philosophy. We actively coach parents so skills learned in the clinic flourish naturally at home.
What makes neurodiversity-affirming care unique?
We honor your child's authentic identity, focusing on sensory comfort, emotional co-regulation, and functional communication without forcing masking or compliance.

Wondering where to begin?

Schedule a friendly introductory consultation with our developmental specialists. Let's explore your child's unique strengths and how we can support your journey together.
