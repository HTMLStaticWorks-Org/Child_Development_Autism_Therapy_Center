Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our services

Have questions about developmental milestones, session formats, or insurance? We are here to help.

What age groups does Bright Path support?
We provide specialized developmental support for infants and toddlers from 12 months up through adolescents aged 16 years. Our primary focus areas include early intervention (1–5 years) and school-age sensory and communication integration (6–14 years).
Do we need a formal diagnosis or referral before scheduling?
No formal diagnosis or doctor referral is required for an initial discovery call or developmental screening. Many families come to us when they first notice speech delays or sensory sensitivities. We conduct thorough neurodevelopmental profiles to guide care.
Can parents participate in the therapy sessions?
Absolutely! We actively encourage parent participation. Our family-centered model includes caregiver coaching during sessions so you can confidently carry sensory and communication strategies over into everyday home life.
How are progress milestones tracked and shared?
Through our secure Parent Portal, you receive weekly clinical notes, milestone percentage progress bars, video demonstrations of home sensory exercises, and quarterly multidisciplinary review reports.
What does "Neurodiversity-Affirming" mean at Bright Path?
It means we respect autism and sensory processing differences as natural neurological variations rather than defects. We do not force eye contact, eliminate safe stimming, or demand mask-like compliance; instead, we build functional communication, comfort, and autonomy.
