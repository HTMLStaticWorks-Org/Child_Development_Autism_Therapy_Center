Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions from parents

Learn more about how our clinical team supports your child's developmental path.

How does Home 2 multidisciplinary care coordinate therapy?
Our occupational therapists, speech pathologists, and pediatric behavioral specialists collaborate under one unified treatment plan. You don't have to manage fragmented advice from multiple clinics.
What is Gestalt Language Processing (GLP)?
Many neurodivergent children learn language through whole phrases or "scripts" (gestalts) before breaking them down into single words. Our speech therapists are certified to nurture gestalt processors toward spontaneous, flexible communication.
How do you support sensory sensitivities during appointments?
Our clinical suites use dimmable warm lighting, noise-absorbing materials, tactile exploration zones, and deep-pressure options. We always pace sessions to your child's nervous system comfort.
