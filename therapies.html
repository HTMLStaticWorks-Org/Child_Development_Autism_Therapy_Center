Clinical Domains

Holistic support for neurodivergent children

Every therapy session is structured as an inviting, child-led exploration designed to develop functional everyday competencies without punitive conditioning.

Communication

Speech & Language Therapy

Targeted assistance for speech delay, apraxia, articulation, functional language acquisition, gestalt processing, and AAC device integration.

Sensory & Motor

Occupational Therapy (OT)

Evidence-based sensory integration strategies, body awareness, fine motor pincer grasp, handwriting readiness, and self-care daily living tasks.

Play & Social

Developmental Play Therapy

Child-directed interactive play facilitating emotional co-regulation, flexible thinking, reciprocal engagement, and deep peer connections.

Family Guidance

Parent Coaching & Counseling

Empowering parents with proactive sensory accommodations, gentle transition tools, and emotional support for navigating an autism diagnosis.

Sensory Integration

Sensory Gym & Regulation

Specialized physical swings, rock climbs, foam pits, and weighted tools designed to recalibrate vestibular and proprioceptive systems.

Cognitive

Cognitive & Executive Function

Support for task initiation, sequential planning, working memory, and mental flexibility tailored for autistic learning profiles.

The Care Pathway

A compassionate, clear journey from day one

  • 1. Comprehensive Intake: We listen to your family history, concerns, and observe your child in a relaxed setting.
  • 2. Multidisciplinary Assessment: Speech, sensory, and developmental evaluations synthesized into one clear profile.
  • 3. Collaborative Goal Mapping: Together, we define functional milestones that bring immediate joy and ease at home.
  • 4. Dynamic Therapy & Reviews: Transparent session notes, continuous reviews, and home sensory diet updates.
