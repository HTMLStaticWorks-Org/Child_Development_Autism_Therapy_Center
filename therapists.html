Integrated Care Model

Why multidisciplinary collaboration matters

At Bright Path, your child doesn't just see individual therapists in isolation. Our speech pathologists, occupational therapists, and pediatric consultants collaborate weekly on each child's unified sensory and developmental plan.

  • Co-treated sessions combining sensory regulation with speech goals
  • Unified communication protocols used across clinic, school, and home
  • Regular case conferences with parents to celebrate progress
  • Direct collaboration with your child's teachers and pediatricians
