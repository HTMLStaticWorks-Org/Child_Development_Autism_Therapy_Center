Our Intake Process

What to expect when you contact us

We understand that reaching out can feel overwhelming. Our process is designed to be gentle, reassuring, and completely unhurried.

📞

1. Warm Intake Call

Our care coordinator speaks with you to listen to your priorities, your child's daily challenges, and what kind of support you envision.

🧩

2. Gentle Play-Based Visit

We invite you and your child for a relaxed, pressure-free orientation in our sensory room to help your child feel safe and at ease.

🌱

3. Collaborative Roadmap

We co-create a clear therapy plan with transparent milestones, home sensory strategies, and consistent therapist pairing.
